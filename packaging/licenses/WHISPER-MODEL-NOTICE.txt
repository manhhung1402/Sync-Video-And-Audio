Whisper model notice
====================

The full installer contains the CTranslate2 conversion of
openai/whisper-small in assets/models/small.

The model card identifies the converted model as MIT-licensed. The model
is derived from OpenAI's whisper-small model; review both upstream model
cards before redistributing a modified model or changing the model size.

- https://huggingface.co/Systran/faster-whisper-small
- https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-small
- https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper
- https://github.com/OpenNMT/CTranslate2
